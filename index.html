Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This is a personal, self-hosted application that lets its owner view their own WHOOP data. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With your permission through WHOOP's OAuth sign-in, the app reads recovery, cycle, sleep, workout, profile and body measurement data from the WHOOP API.

How data is stored

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer in a local database. It is not uploaded to any server and is not shared with or sold to any third party. Access tokens are stored locally and are never shared.

How data is used

Data is used only to show charts, trends and summaries to the person it belongs to.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect at any time from the app's settings, which revokes access with WHOOP and deletes the local data. You can also revoke access from your WHOOP account.

Contact
